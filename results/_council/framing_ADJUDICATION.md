## The disagreement, and the call

**Codex and I independently concluded the headline should flip to zero-shot BPB** (nearly free;
adaptation not shown better). **Fable said no, and Fable is right. I am overturning my own
derivation.**

The argument that decides it: under **lenient**, zero-shot BPB **does not clear chance** —
`0.750 [0.417, 1.000]`, fourth of six, below HellaSwag. Recommending it as *the* selector therefore
violates the both-conventions rule in exact mirror image of the claims we forbid elsewhere. Codex's

rather than on our evidence — and Codex's own escape clause ("unless adaptation answers a
materially different question") is satisfied, so the two seats are closer than they read.

**Adjudicated position:** recommend adapted BPB as *the instrument this study validated* — the only
non-benchmark selector clearing chance under both conventions — and say in the same paragraph that

convention only. Give the reader both facts; let cost decide. Neither tier gets a clean headline,
and pretending otherwise is what the rule exists to prevent.

## What verification changed — this constrains all three seats, including me

Codex and I both reached for a "two different questions" rescue (zero-shot = best model *now*;
adapted = best model *after fine-tuning*), and Fable leaned on a softer version of it.
**It is not supported.** The cloze criterion is generated by the **released base weights** —
verified directly in `results/cloze/cloze__*.json`, whose `model` path is
`models--LiquidAI--LFM2.5-1.2B-Base`. So the criterion measures **base-model in-domain ability
now**. This study never measured post-fine-tuning ability, so "adapted BPB answers the
post-fine-tuning question" is an *interpretation*, not a validated claim, and must be labelled as
one.

Two consequences, and the first is a genuine positive that no seat noticed:

## Exposition plan (merged; Codex and Fable converged here and I adopt it)

1. **Lead with the benchmark null.** GSM8K 0.359/0.410 and MMLU-Pro 0.400/0.450 clear chance under
   neither convention. Rock solid, needs none of our contested numbers, motivates everything after.
2. **One two-panel figure carries the paper**: left, six selectors × two conventions with a chance
   line at 0.5; right, the three paired contrasts against a zero line, with adapted−zero-shot
   labelled **post-hoc**. Reader should not have to reconcile two tables.
3. **Three-way partition as the result**: clears both {adapted BPB, HellaSwag} · clears one
   {zero-shot, size} · clears neither {MMLU-Pro, GSM8K}.
4. **Evidence hierarchy in three short paragraphs**: what is established (clears chance, seed-stable)
   · what comparative advantage is established (lenient over size only) · what is unresolved
   (benchmarks, and the tier question).
5. **Fixed verdict vocabulary**: "clears chance" for marginal, "resolved / unresolved" for paired.
   Delete every "beats".
6. **Decision rule early, >2× exclusion first** — leading with when *not* to use the method buys
   more credibility than any margin.
7. **Demote to appendix** everything that is not the selector figure, rotation, or the robustness
   trio (seed / host / tokenizer).
8. **Plain-language glosses once**: interval, clears chance, in-band. Bootstrap details to a
   methods footnote.
9. **Power story once, in the conclusion.** Restricted to lenient — strict `+0.000` is a null, not a
   power shortfall, and saying otherwise is the one place this paper could still overclaim.

## Falsifier

## The sell, converged — why adapted BPB and not any other way

Adapted BPB is the only selector tested that has **all three** of these; every alternative fails at
least one:

| selector | unpreparable | clears chance under BOTH | reproduces across hosts/seeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **adapted BPB** | **yes** | **yes** (0.900 / 0.850) | **yes** (0.01%; 0 of 40 reorder) |
| zero-shot BPB | yes | no (0.750 lenient) | yes |
| HellaSwag | **no** (public, preparable) | yes (0.825 / 0.825) | not measured |
| parameter count | yes | no (0.650 lenient) | yes |
| MMLU-Pro | no | no | not measured |
| GSM8K | no | no | **no** (−0.60 to +0.69 across builds) |

Stated as a conjunction, deliberately: it is *not* the only unpreparable selector (zero-shot shares
that), and we do not assert HellaSwag is host-unstable — we never measured it. The claim is the
intersection, and the intersection is a set of one.

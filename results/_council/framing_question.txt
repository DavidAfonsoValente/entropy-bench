=== WHAT THE METHOD IS ===

You want to know which newly released base model to fine-tune for your domain. Our proposal:
take a recent sample of YOUR OWN target-domain text, adapt each candidate base model on it under
one fixed budget (250 steps, LoRA, identical for every model), measure Bits Per Byte (BPB =
cross-entropy per byte, tokenizer-agnostic so models with different vocabularies compare on equal
footing) on a held-out split, and pick the lowest. Cost is roughly 1 GPU-hour per candidate.

Call this ADAPTED BPB. The cheap variant, ZERO-SHOT BPB, skips the adaptation and just measures
BPB of the released weights on your text. That one is nearly free.

=== HOW WE VALIDATED IT ===

Ground truth is an IN-DOMAIN CLOZE criterion built from each corpus's own held-out split: hide an
entity or number, give 1,200 characters of context, require the model to GENERATE the answer and
string-match it. Never log-likelihood — a likelihood criterion would agree with BPB by
construction. Because base models ramble, we score two ways and both ship: LENIENT (answer appears
anywhere in a short window) and STRICT (exact prefix). Our standing rule is that any claim
comparing selectors must hold under BOTH.

We score each candidate selector by PAIRWISE SELECTION ACCURACY: over pairs of models within 2x of
each other in parameter count (the case where the choice is genuinely hard), how often does the
selector pick the model the cloze criterion says is actually better? 17 models, 40 such in-band
pairs. Intervals are a cluster bootstrap over models, 20,000 resamples, all selectors scored on
the SAME resampled cohort so paired differences are valid.

=== OTHER EVIDENCE THAT BEARS ON THE FRAMING ===

1. ROTATION (the paper's mechanism, 11 models, 4 corpora, 24 settings): adaptation does not
   improve the model ranking, it ROTATES it toward whatever the adaptation corpus exercises. Which
   corpus you adapt on is therefore the question you are asking. Holds in 23 of 24 settings.
2. RANK ERROR (11 models, news): mean absolute rank error against the cloze ordering is 2.00
   positions for zero-shot BPB and 0.36 for adapted. Zero-shot gets 2 of 11 models exactly right,
   adapted gets 7 of 11. Models zero-shot most underrates: gemma-4-12B, gemma-4-31B, LFM2.5-1.2B.
   NOTE: the artifact itself carries the caveat "a described pattern with named instances, not an
   estimate; the correlations place the pattern and are not tested."
3. SEED ROBUSTNESS: 17 models x 3 seeds. Within-model SD of adapted BPB is 0.000087 vs
   between-model 0.074340 (ratio 0.0012). ZERO of 40 in-band pairs reorder across seeds. The
   +0.250 interval moves by 0.001 when the bootstrap also resamples seeds.
4. HOST FRAGILITY OF BENCHMARKS: identical released weights move -0.60 to +0.69 GSM8K points
   across inference hosts/builds — larger than most effects at stake. BPB pools across hosts
   (a control cell reproduced to 0.00% zero-shot / 0.01% adapted on a third host); generative
   benchmark scores do not.
5. UNPREPARABILITY: the corpora are post-release scrapes of news/Reddit/Hacker News, so no vendor
   could have trained on the specific test text. Public suites can be prepared for.
6. SIZE IS A STRONG FREE BASELINE: over a 70x parameter range, parameter count selects about as
   well as we do (0.855); it only degrades within 2x (0.545 lenient at 11 models).
7. At ELEVEN models nothing resolved anywhere; at forty pairs two selectors clear chance. We frame
   this as a power story.
